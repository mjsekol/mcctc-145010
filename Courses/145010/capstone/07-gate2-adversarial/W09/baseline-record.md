## 1. The question

**B2.** How many repairs stall on an open-shop night because a parts bin is empty?

**B3.** Where it came from: needs-discovery meeting record, Week 7, Friday: "Some nights two or three
repairs stall. Some nights none."

## 2. The method

**B4.** What is counted: repairs marked "waiting on part" in the paper repair log where the note
says the bin was empty.

**B5.** The source: the co-op's paper repair log.

**B6.** The sample: the last six open-shop nights before Week 9, Tuesday.

**B7.** Who took the measurement, and where: the shop coordinator, at the co-op, with the customer
names covered. The coordinator sent the counts only, by school email (contact log L36).

**B8.** Tools used: the coordinator's tally on paper; my arithmetic below.

**B9.** How personal data was kept out: I never saw the log. The coordinator covered the names and
sent six numbers.

## 3. The results

| Line | Open-shop night | Repairs stalled by an empty bin |
|---|---|---|
| B10 | Night 1 | 4 |
| B11 | Night 2 | 2 |
| B12 | Night 3 | 3 |
| B13 | Night 4 | 0 |
| B14 | Night 5 | 5 |
| B15 | Night 6 | 3 |

**B16.** Total: 4 + 2 + 3 + 0 + 5 + 3 = 17 stalled repairs in 6 nights.

**B17.** Per night: 17 / 6 = 2.83, which is 2.8 to one decimal place.

**B18.** Reported as a count: 17 stalled repairs in 6 nights, about 2.8 per night. Not a
percentage, because six nights is a small sample.

## 4. The limits

**B19.** Six nights. They may not be typical of a busier or quieter season.

**B20.** Counted once, by one person, from handwritten notes.

**B21.** A stall is only counted if the volunteer wrote "bin empty." Some stalls may not have been
written down at all, so 17 is a floor, not an exact figure.

## 5. What I did not measure, and why

**B22.** How long each bike waited. The log does not record it reliably.
