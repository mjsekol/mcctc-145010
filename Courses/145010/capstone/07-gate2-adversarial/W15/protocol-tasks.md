## The project, in one paragraph

Garden Watering Sign-Up is a small web app for the Northside Community Garden. Members sign up
for evening and morning watering slots, cancel slots they cannot make, check which beds are driest
this week, and read care notes for each bed. It replaced a paper sheet on the tool shed door.
Stakeholder: the garden coordinator.

## Requirements the tasks test

| ID | Requirement, from the signed requirements specification |
|---|---|
| R1 | A member can sign up for an open watering slot in the current week. |
| R2 | A member can cancel a slot they signed up for. |
| R3 | The home page shows which beds need water first this week. |
| R4 | Bed care notes can be read on a phone-width screen with no sideways scrolling. |
| R5 | Every task can be completed with a keyboard alone, and every page works at 200 percent zoom. |

**Who the users are, from the requirements:** garden members of every age, on their own phones,
a home computer, or the public library computer. The coordinator estimates about forty active
members.

## Starting state for every session

Signed in as the invented test account **Test Member 3**. The Schedule page is open. Tuesday 6 pm
and Thursday 7 am of the current week are open. The garden has twelve beds, and beds 4 and 9 are
marked dry this week.

## Tasks

### Task 1
Read aloud: "You can water the garden this Tuesday evening. Sign yourself up."
Completed when: the Tuesday 6 pm slot of the current week shows **Taken: you**.
Expected time: 1 minute.
Tests requirement: R1

### Task 2
Read aloud: "Something came up and you cannot make Tuesday after all. Take yourself off it."
Completed when: the Tuesday 6 pm slot shows **Open** again.
Expected time: 1 minute.
Tests requirement: R2

### Task 3
Read aloud: "You have ten minutes at the garden today. Find out which beds need water most."
Completed when: the participant names beds 4 and 9.
Expected time: 30 seconds.
Tests requirement: R3

### Task 4
Read aloud: "Put the mouse to one side. Using only the keyboard, sign up for the Thursday
morning slot."
Completed when: the Thursday 7 am slot shows **Taken: you**, with no mouse used.
Expected time: 2 minutes.
Tests requirement: R5

### Task 5
Hand over the test phone with the home page open.
Read aloud: "A neighbor asked how much water the tomato beds need. Find the answer on this phone."
Completed when: the participant reads out the tomato bed care note.
Expected time: 1 minute.
Tests requirement: R4

## Debrief questions, after the tasks

1. If you had to do that again tomorrow, what would you do differently?
2. Was there a moment you were not sure what would happen next?
3. What would you call the schedule page?
4. Is there anything you expected to find and did not?

Each participant is asked two or three of these. Answers are written word for word and labelled
as opinions.

## Materials

This protocol, one printed observation sheet per participant, the test phone, and a timer.
