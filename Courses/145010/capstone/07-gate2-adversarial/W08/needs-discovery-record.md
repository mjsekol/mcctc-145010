## What they said

*Their words, as close as I could get. Quotation marks mark exact phrases.*

**L7.** "Volunteers fix donated bikes on open-shop nights. A volunteer starts a repair, reaches for
a tube or brake pads or a chain, and the bin is empty."

**L8.** "Then the bike sits on the stand until the next night we are open, or longer."

**L9.** "Nobody tells me. I find out when I walk the shelves on Sunday and text myself a list."

**L10.** "Every bin already has a label on the shelf, like B-01 for the 26-inch tubes."

**L11.** "Our volunteers are everybody from high school kids to retirees."

**L12.** "A lot of the older volunteers do not carry a smartphone. They use the donated desktop by
the door to look things up."

**L13.** "Whatever this is has to work on that desktop by the door. Some of the younger ones would
use their phones."

**L14.** "I do not need anything fancy. I need to know what is empty before Sunday."

**L15.** "I do not want volunteers to need an account. Half of them would never remember a
password."

**L16.** "I also do not want strangers messing with it."

**L17.** "We keep a repair spreadsheet with customer names and phone numbers. That does not leave
the building, and nobody outside the co-op sees it."

**L18.** "We have no money for software. Parts money comes from donations, and it is tight."

**L19.** "The volunteer board approves anything new. I bring it to them, and they usually go with
what I recommend."

**L20.** "After you graduate, I would look after it. I am not a programmer."

**L21.** "Some nights two or three repairs stall. Some nights none. The paper repair log has a
'waiting on part' column, so you could count it. I would cover the names first."

**L22.** "I would know it worked if fewer bikes wait on a part we already had money for."

**L23.** "Closed-toe shoes on the shop floor. You would work from the front desk, not at the
stands."

## What I understood

*My interpretation, labelled as mine.*

**L24.** My reading: the pain is not knowing which bins are empty until Sunday.

**L25.** My reading: the desktop by the door is the device that must work. Phones are a second
device.

**L26.** My reading: volunteers should not sign in, but the coordinator does not want the board
open to anyone at all. I need to ask how open is too open.

**L27.** My reading: customer data is off limits for this project.
