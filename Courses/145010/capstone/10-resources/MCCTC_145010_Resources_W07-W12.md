# Additional Resources · Weeks 7-12
## 145010 Senior Capstone · Define, Measure & Analyze, and the first two Improve sprints

**Why this file exists.** The capstone is your project, so most of what you need is already in this
repository: the specification, the rubric, the templates, and the clinic notes. This file points you
at those first, and then at a small number of free outside sources for the skills the capstone asks
for that were not in Weeks 1 to 6.

**Every outside link is marked Confident or [VERIFY].** **Confident** means the address was known to
exist when this file was written. It does not promise the page still says the same thing. A
**[VERIFY]** link has not been confirmed. Open it before you rely on it, and tell your instructor if it
has moved. **In-repository links are relative to this file, and they all resolve.** Reach for those
first.

**Three rules for every resource here.**

1. **Nothing here asks you to create a commercial AI developer account.** Those services require users
   to be 18 or older, and every model in this program runs on lab hardware. If a resource tells you to
   get an API key, it is the wrong resource for this class.
2. **No resource needs your stakeholder's name, records, or data.** Never paste them into any tool,
   site, or AI assistant.
3. **No sign-up with payment details, ever.** If a site asks for a card, close it.

**4. Occupational Outlook Handbook** · `https://www.bls.gov/ooh/` · **Confident** for the handbook.
**Why this one.** Proposal section 15 asks for a labor rate with a citation, and its "labor cost is
more than a wage" paragraph (1.6.12) asks what an employer pays beyond the hourly pay. This is a free, official, national source.
**How to use it:** find the occupation closest to the work you are doing, write down the figure you
actually read, the name of the page, and when you read it. **Never invent a rate, and never copy a
figure from an AI assistant.** The exact occupation page you use is yours to find; its address is
**[VERIFY]** until you have opened it.

**5. Youth employment rules** · `https://www.youthrules.gov/` · **[VERIFY].** If it has moved, search
the U.S. Department of Labor's site for "youth employment". For Ohio's own rules, your instructor
will point you at the state's official page **[VERIFY]**.
**Why this one.** Proposal section 16 asks you to name one kind of rule that would apply if the project
were a paid job. That is 1.3.7. Cite the official page you read.

**6. The Agile Manifesto** · `https://agilemanifesto.org/` · **Confident.** One page. Read it before
Week 11, because your sprints are an Agile ceremony living inside DMAIC's Improve phase.

**Interactive practice.** Rewrite three weak acceptance criteria from the clinic note, then hand them
to a classmate and ask what they would do to check each one. If they cannot say, rewrite it again.
That is the practice, and it is the one the Week 16 acceptance run depends on.

**Side quest.** None this week. Every spare minute goes to the proposal.
