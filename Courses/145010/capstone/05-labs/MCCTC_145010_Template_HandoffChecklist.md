---

## Why this exists

**A handoff that happens in conversation is forgotten in a month.** This checklist is the record
that everything the stakeholder needs was delivered, tested, and approved, and that you let go of
what should no longer be yours.

**"According to company standards"** means the stakeholder's organization decides what final
approval looks like. Ask them in Week 16. Sometimes it is their signature. Sometimes their manager
must agree. Write down what they told you.

**The item students forget is letting go.** Your accounts, your copies of their data, your access to
their systems. A professional hands over the keys and does not keep a spare.
