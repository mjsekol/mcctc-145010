---

## Why this exists

**Handing something over is a project of its own.** Who gets access. Who is trained. Who they call
when it breaks. What happens if it breaks on a day nobody knows how to fix it. A project that works
perfectly and has no rollout plan is a project that stops being used the first time something
unexpected happens.

**"Timely" means before, not during.** 2.13.2 asks you to communicate the plan in a timely manner.
Your stakeholder reads it on Tuesday so that Thursday's training and Friday's handoff hold no
surprises.

**The failure to avoid is a plan with you in it.** After Week 18 you are gone. Every support line
in this plan names someone at the stakeholder's organization, or says honestly that nobody is
available and what that means.
