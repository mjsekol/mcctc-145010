Harbor Lane Bike Kitchen

Volunteer at Repair Night

You do not need to be a mechanic. You need to be patient and willing to say "let me find out."

What volunteers do

  • Greet people and write their bike on the whiteboard
  • Hand out tools and get them back
  • Show someone how to patch a tube, then let them do it

How to sign up

Come to any Repair Night and ask for the shift lead. Volunteers under 18 need a signed permission slip, which the shift lead has.
