Fix your own bike. We have the tools.

Harbor Lane Bike Kitchen is a volunteer-run workshop. You bring the bike, we lend the tools and show you how. Nobody here will fix it for you, and everybody here will help.

Repair Night

Every Tuesday from 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. Bring:

  • Your bike
  • A lock
  • Anything you already bought for it, like a new tube

What we can help you fix
Flat tires and worn tubes
Brakes that squeal or do not stop
Chains that skip or fall off
Gears that will not shift

Helmets are required in the shop area. We have loaners.

Donate a Bike
1. Check that the frame is not cracked.
2. Bring it during Repair Night.
3. A volunteer tags it and gives you a donation slip.
