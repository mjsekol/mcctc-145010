coordinator do not exist. The notes below are a composite of the kind of material a real
volunteer sends: pasted from three places, typed fast, and not checked. Treat every line as
something a real person wrote and might have gotten wrong.

The medical release form is in this folder as `cedar-hollow-medical-release.pdf`.

## Pasted from the board meeting minutes

Code of conduct, in this order because the refs enforce the first three on the spot:

1. ref's call is final (a lot of our refs are 14 and 15, parents need to hear this)
2. only coaches coach from the sideline
3. stay behind the spectator line, 2 yards back
4. cheer for good play on both teams
5. dogs stay on the parking lot side of the fence
6. leave the park cleaner than you found it

If somebody breaks a rule the ref can pause the game and have the coach deal with it. Second
time in the same game, that person leaves the field area for the rest of the game.

Also from the board: shin guards are not optional anymore. The insurance company says every
player wears them in every game. No shin guards, no play.
