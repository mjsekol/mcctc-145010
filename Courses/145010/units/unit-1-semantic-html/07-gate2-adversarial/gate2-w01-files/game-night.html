Teen Game Night

Every other Friday the Maple Room turns into a game room for ages 13 to 18. Bring a friend, learn a new game, or play in the monthly tournament. It is free.

Schedule

6:00 to 9:00 p.m. in the Maple Room, on the first floor past the front desk.

  • 6:00 p.m. Open tables. Pick any game off the shelf.
  • 7:00 p.m. Tournament bracket starts at the stage.
  • 8:30 p.m. Last round, then everyone helps put games away.

What to bring

  • Your own game, if you want to teach it to someone.
  • A water bottle. Snacks are provided.
  • Your signed permission slip, if you are under 18.

Sign up

Everyone under 18 needs a signed permission slip (PDF, 1 page) on file before their first night.

Questions? Email Jordan, the teen program coordinator.

Hollis Street Community Center. The Maple Room is on the first floor and has step-free access from the front entrance.
