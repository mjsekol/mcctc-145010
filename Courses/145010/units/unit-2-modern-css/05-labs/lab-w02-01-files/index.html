Spring Bracket

Eight teams, three rounds, one trophy. Every match is best of three, after school in Room 114.

Club night

The spring bracket is the club's biggest event of the year. Qualifiers ran for three weeks, and eight teams made the cut.

Every team plays at least one match in front of the whole club. The final is streamed to the library screen, so bring a friend who has never watched a match before.

Officers run the stations, keep score, and settle disputes. Their call is final, and it is always made before the next match starts.

If your team is not in the bracket this spring, come anyway. Open play runs on the back row of stations during every round.

How to join

Any student can join the club. You do not need to be good at anything yet.

  • Come to one open play night.
  • Read the club rules with a parent or guardian.
  • Bring the signed rules page to an officer.

Questions go to officers@eastgate-esports.example.
