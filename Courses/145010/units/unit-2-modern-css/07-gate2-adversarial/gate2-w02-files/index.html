Pine Ridge Teen Makers Fair

One Saturday, one hall, and forty teen makers showing what they built this year. Robots, circuits, costumes, games, and at least one very large cardboard dragon.

Entry is free. Workshops are first come, first served, so sign up at the check-in table when you arrive.

Workshops

  • Solder your first badge

    Room A. Build a blinking LED badge you can keep. Safety glasses provided.

    See the time

  • Cardboard armor

    Room B. Patterns, hot glue, and a lot of cardboard. Wear clothes you can get glue on.

    See the time

  • Intro to 3D printing

    Room A. Design a keychain and watch it print. Prints are ready by the end of the day.

    See the time
